ELDOBOT -- Quickstart

1. Open license.key in Notepad and paste the key you received. Save and close.
2. Double-click START.bat. The bot will open the dashboard at http://localhost:3000
3. In Chrome, go to chrome://extensions, turn on Developer Mode (top right),
   click Load unpacked, and select the extension folder inside this package.
4. Open https://www.eldorado.gg in Chrome and log in normally.
   The extension will auto-sync your session to the bot.
5. In the dashboard, pick your regions, tiers, set pricing, hit Start.

That's it. The bot listens 24/7 -- leave it running.

Issues? Hop in our Discord -- link is in your delivery message.
